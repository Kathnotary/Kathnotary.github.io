Frequently Asked Questions

What can be done by video, and what needs to be in person?

Affidavits, statutory declarations, travel consent letters, the Sworn Statement for a Family Gift of a Used Motor Vehicle (Form 013-1157), and similar sworn documents can be commissioned by secure video anywhere in Ontario. Wills, powers of attorney, and notarizations, including certified true copies, are completed in person.

Will my document be accepted if it is done by video?

Most Ontario organizations accept remotely commissioned documents, but not all are required to. Some documents must be signed in person, depending on the type of document or the requirements of the organization receiving it. Please check with that organization before you book a video appointment. If you are unsure, please contact us to arrange an in-person appointment, and we will help you decide.

How do I book an in-person appointment?

In-person appointments are not booked online. Please call, text, or WhatsApp 437-228-2684, and we will arrange a date, time, and place that suit you: your home, office, or hospital, or a convenient public location in Caledon, Brampton, or Mississauga. A travel fee may apply. Video appointments can be booked online at any time.

What ID do I need?

You will need valid, unexpired government-issued photo identification, such as a driver's licence, passport, or Ontario Photo Card. The name on your ID must match the name on your document. Everyone who is signing must attend and present ID. For video appointments, please also email a clear photo of the front of your ID to kathnotary@gmail.com before your appointment, so it can be compared with the ID you show on camera.

Should I sign my document before the appointment?

No. Fill in your document completely, but leave the signature line blank. You will sign during the appointment in front of Kath Notary.

How do I pay?

Payment is accepted by Interac e-Transfer or by credit or debit card at the end of your appointment. Your completed document is released once payment is received.

What do I need for a video appointment?

You will need a phone, tablet, or computer with a working camera and microphone, a stable internet connection, and a quiet, private space. Your video link is included in your booking confirmation.

Can you draft my will or give me legal advice about my document?

No. Kath Notary confirms your identity and that you understand what you are signing, and then witnesses or commissions your signature. We do not draft wills, powers of attorney, or other documents, nor do we give legal advice on their content. Please have your will or power of attorney prepared before your appointment.

How is my information kept private?

Your documents and ID are used only to verify your identity, complete your appointment, and keep the records that Ontario law requires. ID photos are kept securely with the record of your appointment and are never shared. We never sell or share your information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Privacy Policy

Kath Notary respects your privacy and handles personal information in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Information We Collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and appointment details when you book.
  • The documents you ask us to commission, notarize, or witness.
  • For video appointments, a photo of your government-issued photo identification.
  • Payment confirmation details. We do not store card numbers.

Why We Collect It

We use your information only to verify your identity, complete your appointment, deliver your completed document, collect payment, and keep the records that Ontario law requires. By booking an appointment and sending us your documents and ID, you consent to this use.

How We Store It

Documents and ID photos you email to us are received in our business email account, and are then moved to secure storage and removed from our inbox. Documents are signed through a secure, encrypted e-signature service. Booking details are stored in our calendar and email accounts. All accounts are protected with strong passwords and two-step verification.

How Long We Keep It

  • We keep a secure record of each appointment, including a copy of the completed document and the photo of your ID, for as long as our legal and professional record-keeping obligations require. Records are then securely deleted.
  • Emails containing your unsigned documents are deleted after your appointment.

Sharing

We never sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers needed to complete your appointment (such as our e-signature, calendar, and payment providers), or when required by law.

Your Rights

You may ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, subject to legal record-keeping requirements. To make a request, or if you have any privacy questions, please call 437-228-2684.
